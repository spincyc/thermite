## How it works

1. **Describe** the rooms and devices in the house file.
2. **Render.** thermite writes openHAB's configuration from it.
3. **Try.** A throwaway openHAB boots on the result and reports anything that doesn't load.
4. **Install.** One command puts it in place; openHAB picks it up without a restart.
5. **Use.** The remotes switch rooms, sources and volume.

## What it controls

TVs, streaming boxes, AV receivers, projectors, speakers, and HDMI and USB switchers, each over its own network control. More kinds of devices are on the way.

## Why the name

The name draws on aluminothermic metalworking and the craft of joining useful parts. [Goldschmidt's history](https://www.goldschmidt.com/about-us/history/) records the industrial process in 1895. Our mark is a wide, shallow metal bowl with a restrained fire: pewter, copper and a little heat.
