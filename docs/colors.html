Colors and the bowl

thermite shares one bowl mark and one theme across the web, documentation and terminal. The default combines black and charcoal, pewter edges and warm off-white text. Copper marks interaction; a little amber suggests heat. Light mode uses paper-gray and darker metal. Use a built-in theme, or change only the roles you need.

The bowl

96px48px24px16px

The broad, shallow vessel has a clear metal rim and a small asymmetric flame. One set of paths supplies SVG, PNG icons and terminal exports. The terminal uses braille dots for fire and quadrant blocks for metal; its Home view adds restrained motion, which --still stops.

The name recalls aluminothermic metalworking. Goldschmidt's history dates its industrial process to 1895 and its first tramway welds in Essen to 1899. The bowl is our graphic interpretation of a metalworking vessel: simple parts joined with care.

thermite theme show                          # in your terminal, in your theme
thermite theme export svg --out bowl.svg     # for a page of your own

Your own theme

A theme file names itself, says which theme it starts from, and changes only what it wants. This one keeps the bowl and turns the controls teal:

{
  "$schema": "https://spincyc.github.io/thermite/theme.schema.json",
  "name": "harbor",
  "description": "thermite with a sea-glass accent: the bowl keeps its heat, the controls turn teal",
  "extends": "thermite",
  "dark": {
    "accent": "#4fc1c9",
    "accent-ink": "#04191b",
    "focus": "#9be7ea",
    "hues": ["#4fc1c9", "#7fd18b", "#d2a4ec", "#ffc24a", "#ff8a4c"]
  },
  "light": {
    "accent": "#11707a",
    "accent-ink": "#ffffff",
    "focus": "#0b5960",
    "hues": ["#11707a", "#2e7d32", "#7b3fa0", "#9a6700", "#c2410c"]
  }
}

Check it. thermite tells you about unknown roles and badly written colors, and about text too faint to read on its background:

thermite theme check harbor.json
thermite theme show --theme harbor.json --light
thermite theme export css --theme harbor.json

The contrast it asks for follows WCAG, the web's accessibility guidelines:

  • text on bg: at least 4.5:1
  • text on surface: at least 4.5:1
  • text on raised: at least 4.5:1
  • muted on bg: at least 4.5:1
  • muted on surface: at least 4.5:1
  • accent on bg: at least 3:1
  • accent-ink on accent: at least 4.5:1
  • focus on bg: at least 3:1
  • ok on bg: at least 3:1
  • busy on bg: at least 3:1
  • bad on bg: at least 3:1
  • info on bg: at least 3:1
  • flame on bg: at least 3:1
  • metal on bg: at least 3:1
  • each hue on bg: at least 3:1

Editors that understand JSON Schema complete and check theme files as you type: start the file with "$schema": "https://spincyc.github.io/thermite/theme.schema.json". Download the schema, the thermite theme or harbor.
